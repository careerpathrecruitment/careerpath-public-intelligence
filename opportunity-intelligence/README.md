# CareerPath Opportunity Intelligence public feed

Curated Employment & Skills commercial intelligence combining public procurement, DWP pipeline activity and public prime-provider partner opportunities.

V2 adds CareerPath-specific delivery intelligence: factual recommissioning comparisons, aggregated workforce/mobilisation signals, local delivery context where a geography can be matched safely, and a provider partnership map built from public prime-provider EOIs.

V3 adds Programme Economics and Employment & Skills Framework/DPS Intelligence. Programme Economics reports only published funding mechanisms, payment-model wording, commercial evidence and performance evidence. Framework intelligence identifies framework agreements, DPSs, Dynamic Markets and Open Frameworks in CareerPath's curated Employment & Skills procurement corpus, including lots, published suppliers, dates and source-linked access signals where available.

Opportunity facts remain source-linked. Internal bid scoring, raw vacancy records, win probabilities, margin estimates and commercial recommendations are excluded. Coverage metadata explicitly identifies known source gaps rather than implying complete UK lower-value procurement coverage.
