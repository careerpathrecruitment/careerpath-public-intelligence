# CareerPath Opportunity Intelligence public feed

Curated Employment & Skills commercial intelligence combining public procurement, DWP pipeline activity and public prime-provider partner opportunities.

The public feed exposes free headline opportunity facts only: opportunity, commissioner/prime, programme, stage, geography, published value, deadline and original source, plus limited headline market counts.

Detailed requirements, evaluation/commercial analysis, contract history, change intelligence, provider relevance, local delivery, supply-chain comparison and mobilisation/workforce intelligence are protected premium outputs. Internal bid scoring, raw vacancy records, win probabilities and commercial recommendations are excluded. Coverage metadata explicitly identifies known source gaps rather than implying complete UK lower-value procurement coverage.
